01 · Living Water & Community Farming Project

Clean water should be a foundation, not a luxury.

We install solar-powered water filtration systems and deep-water wells in rural villages. Alongside the water systems, communities receive drought-resistant agricultural kits and support to establish sustainable communal greenhouses.

02 · Resilient Minds Youth & Family Support Program

Creating safe spaces where healing can begin.

We build dedicated Safe Spaces within community centers where trained local counselors provide trauma-informed therapy, group healing circles, and psychological first aid to children and families recovering from poverty or crisis.

1,500+children received direct emotional support
