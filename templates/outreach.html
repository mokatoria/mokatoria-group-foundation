Where we have served

Small acts of care, shared across communities.

Our outreach has included visits to children’s homes in Kenya and India, bringing practical supplies and encouragement.

01 / Kenya24 June 2023

Kitui, Kenya

Kwa Vonza Children’s Home

On 24 June 2023, our team held a donation drive in Kitui, including a visit to the Kwa Vonza Children’s Home. We distributed clothing, food, and clean water to children and families.

ClothingFoodWater
02 / Kenya

Eldoret, Kenya

Huruma Children’s Home

We visited the Huruma Children’s Home in Eldoret, providing food and clothing to the children in its care.

FoodClothing
03 / IndiaLate 2025

India

University Children Home

At the end of 2025, we visited the University Children Home in India, bringing food, clothing, and words of encouragement to the children.

FoodClothingEncouragement
